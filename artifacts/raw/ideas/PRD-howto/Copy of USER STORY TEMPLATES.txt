4 Types of User Stories
Simple
Straightforward and to the point. Great for stories that span a single sprint.
Big-picture and ambitious. Perfect for features that need to be broken down over multiple sprints.
Bigger and broader. Ideal for grouping epics under a common product goal or focus area.
Structured and strategic. Built for Scaled Agile teams focusing on program-level objectives.
