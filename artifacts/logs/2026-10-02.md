## US-062 research done

The user answered the plan's questions: no interviews, internet research only, English and Vietnamese, real Hanoi rates for the usual and the extreme case, and the understanding that in Vietnam the flood arrives about 30 minutes after the rain starts. The plan got a Revision 1. I ran about 20 searches and read 12 sources. The findings are in [[flood-map-reaction-time-findings]]. In short: a usual 30-minute burst (about 35 to 62 mm) floods Hanoi streets within minutes, with 20 to 60 cm of water and up to 1 m in a low alley, and it recedes in 30 minutes to 2 hours. The extreme event of 14 to 17 September 2026 (473 to 600 mm cumulative) built over hours. The "30 minutes" in the Vietnamese reports is the burst length and the recession time, not the delay to flooding. No source gives a measured cm per minute for Hanoi. A 15-minute map cannot show a burst that starts after its run, and the nowcast skill for convective storms is 15 to 20 minutes (a 2018 preprint), so the rain forecast is the weak link, not the refresh interval. Recommendation: keep 15 minutes and show the age of the map. `US-062` is `Reviewed` until the user decides.
