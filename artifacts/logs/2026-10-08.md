## Two questions about the design

You asked whether simulation and routing are already separate DAGs. They are: `flood_mapping_full` simulates, and `routing_adapter` runs `load_road_segments`, `flood_on_roads`, and `9c_build_edge_depth_table`. `routing_adapter` has no schedule and runs only on the asset event from `9b_load_depth_raster`, so the core must run for the timing stories to have data. After the replay, pausing `flood_mapping_full` is enough, because `routing_adapter` then stops on its own.

You also asked whether visualization should be a separate container. `flood-routing-frontend`, `floodmap-backend`, `graphhopper`, and the `storage-frontend` and `storage-backend` pair are already their own containers. What they share is the data: one Postgres, one MinIO, and one compose project. The read-only `routing_reader` role and the output contract already fence the data. A separate stack is worth its disk and its second set of 3Di runs only when real users hit the map during a replay. No change was made.
