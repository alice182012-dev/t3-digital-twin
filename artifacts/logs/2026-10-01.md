## US-047 done

The offline reference and checker for sticky C-lite are in `scripts/routing_reference/` and documented in [[sticky-routing-reference]]. `StickyClosure` closes a road when its depth reaches the vehicle limit at any step from the departure to the arrival plus 10 minutes. `earliest_arrival` is Dijkstra over arrival times, and `find_crossings` re-walks a route with per-edge times. There are 54 tests, and the search agrees with an exhaustive search on 300 random graphs. Three deliberate changes to the rule (window end, limit comparison, departure bound) each fail a test. On the real pair 21.0250, 105.7550 to 21.0255, 105.7620 (15 edges, 1,488 m on `marie-curie/raster-first/roads.geojson`), all 16 runs match the sticky column with no crossing, and the plain shortest route crosses a closed road in the 8 blocked runs. Two departures from the story's wording. The code is in `scripts/routing_reference/` because `marie-curie/` is not tracked, and no PostGIS is used because the scenarios need no spatial query. Evidence: `us-047-reference-check-output.png`. Sprint 5 has `US-040` and `US-047` done.
