## Correction: the four skipped runs are a P0 blocker (BL-028)

The user corrected the reading above: this is a storm, and the flood stands after the rain stops, so a gate that stops the simulation is the fault. Logged as [[BL-028-rain-gate-stops-simulation-while-the-flood-still-stands|BL-028]] (P0) with story [[US-101-keep-simulating-while-the-flood-still-stands|US-101]] in Sprint 12.

The mechanism, read from `dags/flood_mapping_dag.py`, `scripts/create_simulation.py`, `scripts/hotstart_policy.py`, and the Airflow task logs of 2026-10-09:

1. `evaluate_rain_gate` looks only at the forecast rain in the lookahead window. The four skipped runs logged 4.52, 4.52, 3.57, and 3.57 mm/h against the 5 mm/h gate.
2. A skip ends the DAG run as `success` and makes no tables, so `routing_adapter` never runs and `latest` stays on `20261009T193000`.
3. The run at 13:30 Z (20:30 +07) printed "Chain check: hotstart allowed", because the skipped run counts as `success`, and then "No saved state within 0:30:00", so it started cold from a dry model.
4. Same clock time, 13:45 Z. The table before the gap (`20261009T193000`, step 5) forecast 3,939 edges with water, 1,614 at 20 cm or more, maximum 656 cm. The table after the gap (`20261009T204500`, step 0) has 1,185, 254, and 310 cm. The size of the true error is not proven, because no continuous run exists to compare.

Consequences: the storm sample of `US-091`, `US-092`, and `US-093` has a one-hour gap and a cold start at 20:30 +07, so their final runs wait for `US-101`. The options (gate on standing water, a hold-down time, or a lower threshold while the flood stands) are in the blocker, and none is chosen. A second fault: the chain check counts a skipped run as `success`, so its message does not match what happens.

## Fix for BL-028 built locally (US-101)

The user decided the rule: the 5 mm/h gate applies only from a dry state, and once the simulation is on it runs until the newest 5 runs of `flood_road_values` are all under 5 cm and the forecast rain is 0. Built on `modular`, not committed, not on the server:

- `scripts/standing_flood.py` holds the rule and the query. `assess` takes the newest runs and the forecast, and `read_standing_flood` reads the newest 5 `flood_road_file` runs with their deepest value. A run is in progress while the newest is under 45 minutes old, and runs more than 30 minutes apart are different chains.
- `task_check_forecast_rain` in `dags/flood_mapping_dag.py` runs the old forecast test first. Only when it says no, it reads the flood. `FLOOD_DRY_DEPTH_M` (default 0.05 m) sets the floor.
- 12 new tests in `scripts/` and 3 gate tests in `test/test_flood_mapping_dag_graph.py` pass. The 6 failures in `test/test_flood_rain_gate.py` are the same before and after.
- Read-only check on the replay tables: at the first skipped slot (12:30 Z, forecast 4.52 mm/h) the rule continues, because the newest runs hold about 6.5 m.

The user accepted the two concerns: a simulation that stays on while the model keeps water above 5 cm is a model issue, not a backend issue, and `flood_road_values` stays as the source because the check must look at roads only, even though `routing_adapter` writes it. Both are in the concerns of the blocker.
