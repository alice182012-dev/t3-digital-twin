## Decisions

Started the [[decision-log]] and recorded D-001 to D-006. The one that changed most:

- **D-006 supersedes D-002 and D-005.** `depth` holds the deepest 1 m sample of a road piece. There is no `depth_max` column and
  the mean is not stored. On two real runs the mean missed 39 to 168 closures per vehicle and run, and the maximum missed 0 to 5
  (9 in total, all beside a piece boundary). The change also removed the schema-first deploy hazard and settled which depth the
  metrics use.
